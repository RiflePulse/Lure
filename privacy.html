LurkVR Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Summary

LurkVR is a virtual reality game developed by RiflePulse. LurkVR is designed to provide a VR gaming experience and does not currently require users to create an account or provide personal information directly to the game.

1. Information We Collect

LurkVR does not currently intentionally collect or store personal information such as your name, email address, postal address, telephone number, or payment information through the game.

LurkVR may process information required for the game to function on your VR device, such as headset and controller tracking data. This information is used for gameplay and VR interaction and is not intentionally stored by LurkVR as a personal profile.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by LurkVR is used only for purposes necessary to operate and improve the game, including providing VR movement, controller input, gameplay functionality, and other features of the experience.

3. Information Stored by LurkVR

LurkVR does not currently maintain a user account system or a personal user database.

If future versions of LurkVR introduce features that collect or store personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are used.

4. Data Deletion Requests

LurkVR does not currently store personal user information in a user account or personal database, so there is currently no personal account data stored by LurkVR that requires deletion.

If LurkVR begins storing personal information in the future, users will be provided with a method to request deletion of that information.

For questions about privacy or data handled by LurkVR, users may contact the developer through the LurkVR project repository.

5. Third-Party Services

LurkVR may be distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta may process information according to its own privacy policies and terms when users interact with Meta services, devices, or the Meta Horizon Store.

LurkVR does not control information collected directly by Meta through Meta's services.

6. Children's Privacy

LurkVR does not knowingly request personal information from users through the game.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when LurkVR's features, data practices, or distribution platforms change.

The latest version will always be available at this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions regarding LurkVR, please use the LurkVR project repository on GitHub.
