LurkVR Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

LurkVR is currently in beta testing.

LurkVR is actively being developed. This means features and data practices may change as new systems are introduced. This policy will be updated when necessary.

1. Information We Collect

LurkVR does not currently intentionally collect or store personal information such as a user's name, email address, postal address, telephone number or payment information directly through the game.

The game may process information required for VR functionality, such as headset and controller tracking information, so that VR movement and interaction can function.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by LurkVR is used only for purposes necessary to operate the game and provide its gameplay and VR functionality.

3. Information Stored by LurkVR

LurkVR does not currently operate its own personal user-account database.

If future versions introduce systems that store personal information, this policy will be updated to describe what is stored and why.

4. Data Deletion

LurkVR currently does not maintain personal user accounts or a personal database containing user information.

If LurkVR begins storing personal user data, a specific method for requesting deletion will be provided in this Privacy Policy.

5. Meta Horizon Platform

LurkVR may be distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta may independently process information through its devices, services and platform. Information processed directly by Meta is governed by Meta's applicable policies.

6. Third-Party Services

LurkVR does not currently intentionally send personal information to third-party services operated by the development team. If this changes, this policy will be updated.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may change as LurkVR develops and new features are introduced. The latest version will always be available on this page.

8. Development Team

LurkVR is developed by RDT — RiflePulse Development Team .

9. Contact

For privacy questions concerning LurkVR, please use the official contact method provided by the LurkVR development team.
