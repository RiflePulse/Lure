L.U.R.E

Privacy Policy — Last Updated: September 12, 2026
IMPORTANT: L.U.R.E is currently in beta testing. This Privacy Policy describes the current way L.U.R.E and RDT handle information.

1. About L.U.R.E

L.U.R.E is a virtual reality game developed by RDT — RiflePulse Development Team.

This Privacy Policy applies to the L.U.R.E game, its related services and the official L.U.R.E website.

2. Information We Collect

L.U.R.E is currently in beta and does not currently operate its own player account database.

RDT does not intentionally request information such as your real name, home address, telephone number or payment information directly through the game.

VR / DEVICE INFORMATION

L.U.R.E may process information required for the game to function on a supported VR device, including headset and controller tracking information used for gameplay.

This information is used to provide the VR experience and is not intentionally used by RDT to create a personal profile of the player.

3. How Information Is Used

Information required by the game may be used to:

  • Provide VR gameplay functionality.
  • Track player movement required for gameplay.
  • Allow controllers and headset tracking to function.
  • Improve stability and development of L.U.R.E.

4. Third-Party Platforms

L.U.R.E may be distributed through Meta's platform. Meta may process information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

RDT does not control information that Meta independently processes through the Meta Horizon platform.

5. Website

The L.U.R.E website is primarily an informational website for the game and RDT.

The website does not currently provide an RDT account system or direct payment system.

6. Developer Applications

Applications to join RDT may be submitted through the official developer application form.

Information submitted through that form is provided voluntarily by the applicant and is used for reviewing applications to the RDT development team.

The developer application form is hosted by Google, which may process submitted information according to Google's own policies.

7. Beta Testing

L.U.R.E is currently in beta testing. Beta testing may involve external Meta services or testing systems provided by the platform.

Information processed by those external services may be governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Data Deletion Requests

Because L.U.R.E currently does not maintain its own player account database, RDT does not currently have a player-account deletion system.

If you believe RDT has received or stored personal information relating to you and would like to request deletion, submit a request through the official RDT developer application form and clearly state:

"PRIVACY / DATA DELETION REQUEST"

Include enough information for RDT to identify the relevant submission without providing unnecessary personal information.

If L.U.R.E introduces accounts, analytics, multiplayer services or other systems that store additional personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain those changes and the applicable deletion process.

9. Children's Privacy

L.U.R.E is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children through its own account system.

Platform age requirements and parental controls may still apply depending on the platform used to access L.U.R.E.

10. Changes To This Policy

RDT may update this Privacy Policy when L.U.R.E's features, services or data practices change.

The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Development Team

RDT — RiflePulse Development Team

RDT is the development team responsible for L.U.R.E.
