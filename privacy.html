LurkVR Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

LurkVR is currently in beta testing.

LurkVR is actively being developed, so this Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's data practices or features change.

1. Information We Collect

LurkVR does not currently intentionally collect or store personal information such as a user's name, email address, postal address, telephone number, or payment information through the game.

LurkVR may process information required for VR gameplay, such as headset and controller tracking information. This information is used to provide VR movement and interaction and is not intentionally stored by LurkVR as a personal profile.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by LurkVR is used only as necessary to operate the game and provide gameplay features, including VR movement, controller input, and other interactive features.

3. Information Stored by LurkVR

LurkVR does not currently operate its own user account system or personal user database.

If future versions introduce features that collect or store personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those practices.

4. Data Deletion

LurkVR does not currently maintain personal user accounts or a personal database containing user information.

If LurkVR begins storing personal user information in the future, this policy will provide a specific method for requesting deletion of that information.

5. Meta Horizon Platform

LurkVR may be distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta may independently process information through its devices, services, and platform.

Information processed directly by Meta is governed by Meta's applicable privacy policies and is not controlled by LurkVR.

6. Third-Party Services

LurkVR does not currently intentionally send personal information to third-party services operated by the developer.

If this changes in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant data processing.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as LurkVR develops and new features are introduced.

The current version will always be available on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions concerning LurkVR, please use the contact method provided by the developer on the LurkVR project page.
