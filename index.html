About LurkVR

LurkVR is a virtual reality horror game developed by RiflePulse. The game takes place inside a mysterious research facility filled with strange locations, unexplained events, and things that may not want you there.

Explore the facility, investigate what happened, and survive whatever is waiting in the darkness.

Features

VR Locomotion

Physical VR movement designed around immersive hand-based movement.

Research Facility

Explore a mysterious facility containing different environments and hidden areas.

Horror Events

Unexpected events and encounters can change the atmosphere around you.

Exploration

Search the facility, discover secrets and uncover more of the LurkVR world.
