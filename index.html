WELCOME TO LURKVR

LurkVR is a virtual reality horror experience built around exploration, mystery and the unknown. You have entered a facility that was never meant to be discovered. Something happened here. The records are incomplete. The rooms don't always make sense. And some things inside the facility were never meant to leave.

THE PARADOX

"The deeper you go, the less you understand."

The Paradox is not simply another area of the facility. It is something that should not exist. Doors lead somewhere different. Rooms appear where they shouldn't. Information has been removed from the records. And somewhere beyond the parts marked REDACTED, something is waiting.
