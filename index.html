WHAT IS L.U.R.E?

L.U.R.E is a VR horror project from RiflePulse Development Team. The project combines exploration, atmosphere, environmental storytelling, and unsettling encounters inside a mysterious facility.

01

EXPLORE

Move through different areas of the facility and uncover paths, rooms, records, and clues that gradually reveal what happened.

02

SURVIVE

Stay alert as the facility changes around you. Sound, darkness, restricted areas, and unexpected events are part of the experience.

03

DISCOVER

Piece together the story of PARADOX through environmental details, research files, locations, and discoveries hidden throughout L.U.R.E.

FACILITY DATABASE

THE FACILITY

The facility was built for research that was never meant to become public. What begins as a controlled environment gradually becomes something much harder to understand.


The deeper you go, the less reliable the environment becomes. Doors may lead somewhere unexpected, familiar areas may feel different, and the facility's history is scattered across the places left behind.
